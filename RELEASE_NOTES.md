Double Interlock 1.1.0 adds field calls, design challenges, and a larger, more readable workspace.

- **Eight find-the-fault field calls:** investigate inherited installations and live incidents, diagnose the cause, prove the repair, and compare your layout with the cheapest and maintained fixes. Variants, graduated hints, and three-star scoring reward sound troubleshooting.
- **Eight design calls:** solve budget, part-count, pipe-retention, isolation, and water-supply constraints. A separate progression track, explicit design decisions, and checks of the most demanding hydraulic area make each challenge distinct.
- **Expanded workspace and visuals:** adaptive equipment tabs and inspector, Focus view (`F`), Fit site (`Home`), a campus minimap, zoom controls, optional 5 ft visual guides, richer equipment and fire/water effects, and reduced-motion support.
- **Tactical learning tools:** field notes explain selected equipment and link to the relevant manual chapter; the system monitor keeps alarms and the event log within reach.
- **Commissioning and recovery fixes:** drills preserve the commissioning record, Churn's quiet clock survives saved games, and field-call recovery judges panel resets when they happen. Design-call calculations preserve the full design-area sprinkler count when checking the worst area.
- **License and notices:** the installer now displays the proprietary personal-use license and includes third-party notices. The license and notices are also available as release assets.

Install **Double-Interlock-1.1.0-windows-x64-setup.exe** on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit Intel/AMD), or choose **Check for updates** in the desktop app's contracts menu. No Node.js, Rust, or administrator account is needed. Setup installs WebView2 if it is missing, which needs an internet connection; the game then runs offline. Saved progress stays on this PC across app upgrades. Browser saves are separate from desktop saves.

The installer is unsigned, so Windows may show a SmartScreen prompt. **SHA256SUMS.txt** provides SHA-256 hashes for all release assets. The self-contained **double-interlock.html** is also available for browser play. See **LICENSE.txt** for permitted use.
